Preserving East Africa's History Through Digital Storytelling

Discover untold African historical stories, support documentary filmmaking, and help preserve East Africa's rich cultural heritage for future generations. Transforming oral histories into digital permanence.

What is Lygasi?

Lygasi is an independent digital media and historical preservation initiative dedicated to capturing, documenting, and archiving East Africa's pivotal oral histories and cultural heritage through cinema-grade documentary filmmaking.

Historical Preservation
Transforming vulnerable oral accounts and overlooked historical records into permanent high-definition digital archives.
Independent Storytelling
Empowering East African filmmakers and historians to uncover untold political, social, and cultural stories.
Educational Access
Distributing accessible documentary content and study resources for schools, universities, and global audiences.

Our Focus Areas

Historical Documentaries

Preserving East Africa's untold stories through professional documentary filmmaking and digital archiving.

Cultural Preservation

Documenting traditional practices and cultural narratives before they are lost to time.

Community Engagement

Working with local communities to ensure authentic representation, mentorship, and local storytelling.

Our Mission

Preserving East Africa's rich history through innovative digital storytelling and documentary filmmaking.

The Challenge

East Africa's history is rich, but much of it risks being forgotten or lost. Key historical figures and cultural narratives remain undocumented, under-documented, or inaccessible to the wider public.

  • Without systematic documentation, many contributions to East Africa's identity and heritage will fade.
  • Independent filmmakers face financial challenges in producing long-form documentaries with historical depth.
  • Existing archives are often donor-funded and not easily accessible to the public.

Our Solution

We've created an innovative platform where history meets modern storytelling:

  • Short-form (5–15 mins) and long-form (30+ mins) documentaries are professionally produced and curated
  • Accessible content through modern digital platforms
  • Proceeds fund ongoing historical documentation projects
  • Collaborative approach with local communities and historians

Our Vision

To become the premier digital archive of East African historical narratives, making our shared heritage accessible to generations to come.

  • Build a sustainable platform for historical preservation
  • Create educational resources for schools and institutions
  • Foster cross-generational dialogue about our history
  • Empower local storytellers and filmmakers

Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about Lygasi, our production model, and how to support historical preservation.

What is Lygasi and what is your core mission?

Lygasi is an independent digital media and documentary production initiative. Our core mission is to preserve East Africa's rich historical narratives and cultural heritage by capturing oral histories and historical records into cinema-grade digital documentaries for global audiences and future generations.

What is "Uganda's Gang of Four" documentary?

Uganda's Gang of Four is an upcoming 25-minute feature documentary uncovering the pivotal political figures who shaped modern Uganda during a crucial transitional era. It combines extensive archival research, historical insights, and exclusive firsthand interviews.

How can I sponsor or donate to a documentary project?

We welcome individual and corporate sponsors. You can support our active projects directly via our Selar sponsorship page or choose a sponsorship tier (Seed Sponsor $1,000, Story Champion $2,500, Legacy Partner $5,000) by contacting us directly.

How does Lygasi preserve historical accuracy?

We work directly with academic historians, primary witnesses, cultural elders, and verified institutional archives to ensure thorough fact-checking, contextual depth, and ethical storytelling.

Can educational institutions partner with Lygasi?

Yes! We actively collaborate with schools, universities, and cultural centers to provide educational screenings, historical video resources, and student workshops on oral history recording.
